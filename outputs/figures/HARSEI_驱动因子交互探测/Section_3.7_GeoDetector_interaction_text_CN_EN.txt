【中文正文可用表述】

为进一步识别驱动因子之间的耦合效应，本研究基于最新重建的年度 HARSEI 栅格，采用 GeoDetector 交互探测器计算各驱动因子两两叠加后的解释力 q(X1∩X2)。为避免循环解释，交互探测仅纳入外生环境因子，包括 DEM、SLOPE、ASPECT、WRB、PRE、TEMMAX、TEMMIN、TEM、SOIL、AET 和 SWE；未将 HARSEI 的组成项及 HAI/LUCC 相关变量作为驱动因子。连续变量按年度分位数划分为 7 个等级，WRB 保持为类别变量。交互类型根据 q(X1∩X2) 与 q(X1)、q(X2) 及 q(X1)+q(X2) 的关系划分为双因子增强、非线性增强、独立及减弱类型。

结果表明，各年份驱动因子交互后的解释力普遍高于单因子解释力，说明伊犁-准噶尔盆地生态环境质量的空间分异并非由单一自然因子控制，而是由水热条件、土壤背景和地形约束共同塑造。单因子探测中，AET、PRE、SOIL 和 WRB 在多数年份保持较高 q 值，其中 2000 年 AET、PRE、SOIL 和 WRB 的 q 值分别为 0.745、0.694、0.674 和 0.595；2024 年分别为 0.721、0.700、0.681 和 0.578，表明水分可利用性、降水输入、土壤条件及土壤类型背景是 HARSEI 空间差异的主要基础。

交互探测进一步显示，WRB-AET、WRB-PRE、TEMMAX-AET 和 TEM-AET 等组合在多个年份表现出较强解释力和双因子增强特征。例如，WRB-AET 的交互 q 值在 2000、2005、2010、2015、2020 和 2024 年分别达到 0.826、0.809、0.810、0.798、0.798 和 0.822。该结果说明，土壤类型和实际蒸散共同限定了区域植被生长、盐分迁移及地表水热过程，从而对 HARSEI 空间格局产生复合影响。总体来看，交互探测结果支持“水分-土壤-热量”复合控制机制，即生态质量较高区域通常受较好的水分供给和土壤条件共同支撑，而生态质量较低区域则更易受到干旱、土壤背景及热量胁迫的叠加影响。

【English manuscript-ready wording】

To further identify the coupled effects among driving factors, the GeoDetector interaction detector was applied to the revised annual HARSEI rasters by calculating the explanatory power q(X1∩X2) after pairwise overlay of two factors. To avoid circular interpretation, only exogenous environmental drivers were included, namely DEM, SLOPE, ASPECT, WRB, PRE, TEMMAX, TEMMIN, TEM, SOIL, AET, and SWE. HARSEI component variables and HAI/LUCC-related variables were not used as drivers. Continuous variables were discretized into seven annual quantile classes, whereas WRB was retained as a categorical factor. Interaction types were classified according to the relationships among q(X1∩X2), q(X1), q(X2), and q(X1)+q(X2).

The interaction detector showed that the explanatory power of paired factors was generally higher than that of individual factors, indicating that the spatial heterogeneity of ecological quality in the Ili-Junggar Basin was jointly shaped by hydrothermal conditions, soil background, and topographic constraints rather than by a single environmental factor. In the factor detector, AET, PRE, SOIL, and WRB consistently showed relatively high q values across the study period. For example, their q values were 0.745, 0.694, 0.674, and 0.595 in 2000, and 0.721, 0.700, 0.681, and 0.578 in 2024, respectively. These results suggest that water availability, precipitation input, soil conditions, and soil-group background are major determinants of the spatial differentiation of HARSEI.

The interaction results further revealed strong explanatory power and bivariate enhancement for combinations such as WRB-AET, WRB-PRE, TEMMAX-AET, and TEM-AET in multiple years. In particular, the q values of WRB-AET reached 0.826, 0.809, 0.810, 0.798, 0.798, and 0.822 in 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015, 2020, and 2024, respectively. This pattern indicates that soil type and actual evapotranspiration jointly regulate vegetation growth, salt transport, and land-surface hydrothermal processes, thereby exerting a compound influence on the spatial pattern of HARSEI. Overall, the interaction detector supports a coupled "water-soil-thermal" control mechanism: areas with higher ecological quality are generally sustained by favorable water supply and soil conditions, whereas lower-quality areas are more likely to be affected by the combined constraints of aridity, soil background, and thermal stress.
