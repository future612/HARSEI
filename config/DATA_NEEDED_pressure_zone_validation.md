## 3. Published Northern-Xinjiang / Field Salinity Data

The strongest supplement would be a local or regional salinity dataset, either:

- georeferenced salinity map: `.tif`
- field samples: `.csv/.xlsx` with at least `lon`, `lat`, `year`, and a salinity column
- known saline-area polygons digitized from a published map: `.gpkg/.shp/.geojson`

`D:\Codex\260724 小论文\revise\external_validation_inputs\north_xinjiang_salinity_local`

For point tables, use this schema if possible:

```csv

```

Units can be `g/kg`, `dS/m`, or another published salinity unit, but please keep the unit in the filename or a README.
